Johrei

Johrei is the sharing of Divine Light.

A session is a quiet, still practice offered person to person. It lasts about 15 minutes, and may be received on its own or added to a one-on-one meeting with your minister.

Session Times

15 minutes • requested at least 48 hours ahead • confirmed by your minister

My Johrei Sessions

One-on-One with Your Minister

Meeting with your minister each week is a spiritual practice.

In the teachings of Meishu-sama, we learn to grow stronger through sincere connection (Makoto). A regular, personal conversation is one of the simplest ways to deepen gratitude, find clarity in daily life, and renew your spirit.

Come as you are — with a question, a purification, or simply the wish to be present.

How it works

📅

Request 48 hours ahead

Meetings must be requested at least two days in advance.

✓

Your minister confirms

The date, time and length are approved before the meeting is set.

💬

Online or in person

Online meetings are held on Zoom. The link appears here once approved.

🕐

30 minutes or 1 hour

Choose the length that suits you; your minister will confirm it.

🔔

You'll be reminded

A reminder 24 hours before, and again 1 hour before.

⚠️

Cancel up to 8 hours before

After that, please contact your minister directly.
